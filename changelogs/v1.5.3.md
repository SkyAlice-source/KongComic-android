## English

### KongComic v1.5.3

**Interface**

- Switching bottom tabs is instant now. The outgoing and incoming pages used to overlap in a 320ms cross-fade, so the old page's content visibly bled through the new one for a moment — it looked like the page had not refreshed.
- If you scrolled the bottom navigation bar away and then swiped back, the bar now slides back instead of immediately asking whether to exit. Swipe once more to actually leave the app.
- Fixed the bottom bar sometimes staying hidden after you came back from a comic's detail page (it had been hidden by scrolling).
- The top bar is always visible on root pages, so you still know where you are while the bottom bar is tucked away.

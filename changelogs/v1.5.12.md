### KongComic v1.5.12

- History & favorites redesigned to match the home style: unified tool-pill top bars, grouped menus, one hero "All favorites" card, network favorites folded into the folder selector.
- New "Favorited" capsule badge on covers (shown in discovery/search/recommendations, hidden on your own library pages).
- Menus: dividers, custom rows, grouped sections; history items gain "Add to favorites".
- Fix: grey smudge under the detail top bar on AMOLED (glass blur pulling the cover into the bar while at rest).
- Fix: invisible accent icons in AMOLED (face token misused as icon color).

## English

### KongComic v1.5.21

- Favorites / History toolbars simplified: bare icons on top, filters and actions always visible in two compact rows.
- One shared layout button across Discover, Favorites and History — grid / list in a single tap.
- Centered rounded dialogs with consistent corners; the redundant × is gone (tap outside or Back to close).
- Multi-select outlines each comic separately; pull-to-refresh works on short and empty lists.
- Home cards and search / result / image-favorites pages tuned for large text, narrow screens and tablets.

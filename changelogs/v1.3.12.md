## What's Changed

- **The navigation bar now gets out of the way when you read.** With development paused for some time, this is the fix that answers the open request from issue #3: on wide screens — an unfolded phone, a tablet, or a phone held sideways — the very same navigation bar stops sitting along the bottom and instead stands as a narrow strip down the left side. It stayed there during reading, taking up width and pressing against the comic page. It now slides away the moment you open a reader, leaving the comic centred on the full screen, and slides back when you leave.
- **You can turn that off.** A new switch, *Hide navigation while reading*, sits in the reader settings and is on by default. Turn it off and everything behaves the way it used to — the navigation bar stays put, including during reading.
- **Reading in landscape leaves more room for the page.** The bottom toolbar used to spend two rows on controls, roughly 100dp of vertical space, which on a sideways screen is a large slice of what little height you have. It now lays the progress slider, the page jump and the chapter buttons out on a single row (~56dp), so the image gets the rest.
- **The status bar no longer pops back over the comic when you rotate.** Full-screen reading is set up when the reader opens, but the system quietly resets it whenever the screen rotates, revealing the clock and battery over the page. It is now re-applied on rotation as well.
- **Fixed a flaw in that animation work:** the navigation frame ran two independent fade/slide animations while only being allowed to provide one tick source, so the two fought over the same driver, and the second one never released it. Any follow-up animation after hiding the bar could misbehave; debug builds reported the conflict outright.
- **The reading progress label now follows the app language.** The badge under a cover read something like "第12话 · 45%" regardless of which language you had selected, including Japanese. It reuses the existing translated string and gains proper Japanese wording.
- **Fixed two places where the app held on to resources it no longer needed:** a page controller in the full-screen image favourites view, and the text fields behind two dialogs in Settings → Explore — the latter leaking every time either dialog was closed by tapping outside rather than pressing a button.
- **Update notes now speak your language.** If a release ever comes without a description, the fallback one-paragraph note used to mixes Chinese and English in a single line. It is translated like everything else now.
- **Your data can no longer be pulled out with `adb backup`.** Android applications accept it by default; it means anyone who can plug your phone into a computer could copy the app's private data off it, including your history and collections. This build opts out explicitly.
- **Housekeeping:** the Gradle build file no longer uses assignment syntax that the next major Gradle release removes, and the rules controlling release-time obfuscation were narrowed down to the few entry points that actually need keeping, instead of excluding the entire app from shrinking.
